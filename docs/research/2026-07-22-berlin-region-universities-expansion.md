# Berlin-region English CS master's research log

Reviewed: 2026-07-22

## Scope and decision rule

This pass checked Berlin and the previously selected Berlin commuter cities without imposing a strict journey-time cutoff. A row qualifies when the institution has a physical campus in the region, the programme is a currently advertised on-campus master's related to computer science, it is taught in English, and international applicants can use the published route. Existing TU Berlin, FU Berlin and University of Potsdam/HPI rows were deliberately left untouched.

Admissions, fees and campus claims use institution or DAAD records. Every row has a numeric overall world rank: EduRank 2026 is preferred, while rows absent from EduRank use a clearly disclosed fallback. Subject columns are populated only when a ranking source publishes a direct matching topic and otherwise remain empty. Campus coordinates are approximate neighbourhood pins when an authoritative point coordinate was unavailable, as requested; every pin links to the institution's campus information.

## Commute and spatial assessment

The [VBB RE1 corridor](https://www.vbb.de/fahrinformation/zug-um-zug-mehr-schiene/linienuebersicht/re1/) supports the regional inclusion of Potsdam, Brandenburg an der Havel and Cottbus as rail-connected options. This is evidence of corridor connectivity, not a promise of a fixed door-to-door duration. Timetables, construction diversions, campus transfers and the student's Berlin neighbourhood must be checked before accepting an offer. Berlin rows are locally commutable; Potsdam is the strongest routine cross-city option. Brandenburg an der Havel and especially Cottbus are long-commute choices retained because the user requested a permissive boundary.

## Quality notes

- Admission tags distinguish confirmed future intakes from officially open routes: `Winter ’26 open`, `Summer ’27 open`, `Winter ’27 open`, `Summer ’27`, `Winter ’27`, and `No CS intake ’27`.
- A plain intake tag is not an open-portal guarantee. A green `open` tag means official evidence supported an active route for that named intake on 22 July 2026; capacity and visa feasibility can still change.
- Zero application fee means no separate processing charge was identified for the direct route; it does not mean tuition is zero. Private-university applicants should obtain a written total-cost schedule before enrolment.

## Rank and tuition inclusion rules — 22 July 2026

The final dataset applies two inclusive, conjunctive numeric rules:

- Overall rank must be within 3,200: retain `overallRank <= 3200`.
- Non-EU tuition must be within €5,000 per semester: retain `nonEuTuition <= 5000`.

A university is removed when it fails either rule. The rank rule removed UE (3,208), SRH (6,252), Hochschule Fresenius (7,403), TH Brandenburg (7,663), and Gisma (15,013). The tuition rule additionally removed TUM (€6,000), Hertie (€9,125), and Lancaster Leipzig (€12,500) per semester. Their research notes above are retained as an audit trail rather than active dataset entries. BHT remains because its overall rank is 3,104 and its tuition is €0.

The remaining public programmes' reviewed Winter 2026 international/foreign-degree windows had closed by 22 July 2026. Examples cross-checked against current official pages include FU Data Science (31 May), HPI Computer Science (1 June), Bonn (1 June), BHT (15 June), BTU AI (15 July), HHU AI and Data Science (15 July), and HTW MPMD (explicitly closed). Hertie's Fall 2026 cycle is also closed. No `open` tag was added from a German-degree-only, higher-semester or exchange-student deadline.
