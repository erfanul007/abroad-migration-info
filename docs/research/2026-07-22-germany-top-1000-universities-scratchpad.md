# Germany Top-1,000 English CS University Audit Scratchpad

**Started:** 22 July 2026
**Status:** Active research log; findings are not dataset facts until marked `PASS` and reconciled.

## Decision gates

- Rank: EduRank 2026 overall world rank `<= 1000` nationwide; qualifying-programme campuses in Berlin and the established Berlin-commuter cities use the approved `<= 3200` regional exception.
- Programme: current, degree-seeking and on-campus; computing-centred; completable in English; accepts a CSE-related prior degree and foreign credentials.
- Cost: mandatory Bangladeshi/non-EU tuition `<= €5000` per semester. Semester contribution and application fees are recorded separately.
- Evidence: EduRank for ranking only; official university programme/curriculum, international-admission, fee and campus sources for operational facts. DAAD/Hochschulkompass may cross-check but cannot replace current official rules.

## Per-institution checklist

Each completed entry records: `Decision`, `Rank`, `Programme`, `English path`, `International route`, `Tuition`, `Intakes/window`, `Campus`, `Ownership`, `Subject ranks`, `Conflicts/risks`, and `Sources`.

## 22 July completeness remediation

A row-by-row comparison against the mature pre-existing records found that the 28 additions had complete inclusion evidence but incomplete presentation data: 19 lacked at least four EduRank topic ranks, ten lacked all five, all used broad city-centre pins, and several timelines and narratives were placeholders.

Remediation completed for all 28 additions:

- Recorded the 2026 EduRank Computer Science, AI, Machine Learning, Data Science and Software Engineering topic ranks. Greifswald has four values because EduRank does not publish a matching Software Engineering topic, which is now explicitly disclosed.
- Replaced broad pins with address-level programme/faculty campus coordinates and an explicit address-level accuracy note. These are campus-address pins, not claims about a building entrance.
- Replaced generic timeline text with published cycle dates or an explicit statement that the university has not yet released the relevant 2027 programme deadline.
- Reconciled the intake chips with the qualifying English programme under the Bangladeshi/visa-required lens. Münster, Bielefeld, Bremen, Ulm, Konstanz, Greifswald, Wuppertal, Chemnitz, Oldenburg and Siegen are winter-only in this dataset.
- Reconciled numeric application fees with uni-assist/VPD and university evaluation routes (€75 standard first uni-assist/VPD application; €60 RPTU evaluation; €70 Trier evaluation).
- Added programme-specific academic/language requirements and applicant checks, current semester charges, individual EduRank pages, and map evidence.
- Replaced vague current charges where exact official Winter 2026/27 figures are published: Münster €369.50, Kiel €403 plus €55 first enrolment, Saarland €412.30, and Bremen €425.40.

Automated audit result before the regional exception was 48 rows. The first regional reconciliation produced 52 rows; the 23 July all-listed-city reconciliation produces 56 rows and separately tests every coverage addition for rank, tuition, timelines, map addresses and evidence completeness.
