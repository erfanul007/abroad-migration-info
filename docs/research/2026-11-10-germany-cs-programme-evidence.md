## Academic and application checks for the summer candidates

### TU Dresden — Computer Science

English. Foreign-degree applicants use the university application switch, generally leading to uni-assist, **and** a separate aptitude portal. IELTS minimum is 7.0 overall and 6.0 in every component. The department publishes a conversion formula for local credit hours and asks applicants to avoid double counting. [dresden2026csadmission](https://tu-dresden.de/ing/informatik/studium/studienangebot/master-studiengaenge/m-sc-computer-science/admission)

The required 90 ECTS comprise minimum allocations of 20 systems/networks/databases/security, 10 theory/algorithms/data structures, 20 mathematics, 5 architecture/engineering/HPC, 25 programming/software/HCI/graphics/web, plus 10 across those areas. Classification follows learning outcomes; relevant laboratory content needs documentation. The current regulation requires machine-readable, searchable PDFs for electronically submitted evidence except the degree certificate. [dresden2026csregulations](https://www.verw.tu-dresden.de/Amtbek/PDF-Dateien/2026-13/02_AB26_256_EFO_Computer_Science.pdf)

Official module descriptions or university-certified descriptions are needed; a course title alone is insufficient. The department recommends GRE for non-EU degrees but does not list it as a universal compulsory test on this admissions page. Ordinary degree fees and special fee cases must be distinguished. [dresden2026csadmission](https://tu-dresden.de/ing/informatik/studium/studienangebot/master-studiengaenge/m-sc-computer-science/admission) [dresden2026fees](https://tu-dresden.de/studium/im-studium/studienorganisation/semesterbeitrag-studiengebuehren?set_language=en)

### Leibniz University Hannover — Computer Science

The programme expressly permits completion through English lectures. Applicants can demonstrate English C1 instead of German; the accepted-certificate list includes IELTS Academic 7.0. Entry rules specify 15 ECTS mathematics, 10 theoretical CS, 20 CS fundamentals and 20 advanced CS. [hannover2026cscourse](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/studienangebot/info/studiengang/detail/computer-science-1?limit=all) [hannover2024cslaw](https://www.uni-hannover.de/fileadmin/luh/studium/ordnungen/zugang/englische_uebersetzungen/zo_m_EN_Computer_Science_and_Computer_Engineering.pdf) [hannover2026englishcerts](https://www.llc.uni-hannover.de/en/language-examinations/accepted-language-certificates-at-the-luh)

For the relevant foreign-degree/non-EU route, obtain the LUH-specific VPD and apply through the university portal. The standing summer window ends 11-30. Uni-assist processing time can make a late VPD request impractical; do not assume an admission letter from another university substitutes for this document. Standard semester contributions remain payable; no programme-specific ordinary CS tuition levy was found in the fetched cost guidance. [hannover2026cscourse](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/studienangebot/info/studiengang/detail/computer-science-1?limit=all) [hannover2026vpd](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung-zulassung/voraussetzungen-zum-studium/vorpruefungsdokumentation) [hannover2026costs](https://www.uni-hannover.de/en/studium/finanzierung-foerderung/studienkosten)

### TU Berlin — Computer Science

English; summer and winter entry; open admission. The summer 2027 window is 2026-12-01–2027-02-28 and winter 2027/28 is 2027-06-01–2027-08-31. These are the **open** master's windows, not the restricted English-master deadlines. Foreign-degree applicants need a TU-specific VPD, tuPORT application and the Suitability Assessment Form. [tuberlin2026cs](https://www.tu.berlin/en/studying/study-programs/all-programs-offered/study-course/computer-science-informatik-m-sc) [tuberlin2026csadmission](https://www.tu.berlin/en/eecs/academics-teaching/organizing-your-studies/during-your-studies/msc-computer-science-informatik/msc-cs-in-application-admission) [tuberlin2026foreign](https://www.tu.berlin/en/i-a-office-of-student-affairs/masters-application-enrollment/application/applying-with-a-primary-university-degree-from-a-foreign-university) [tuberlin2026deadlines](https://www.tu.berlin/en/studierendensekretariat/dates-deadlines-for-application-and-enrollment-at-tu-berlin)

Core requirements include 12 ECTS theoretical CS, 12 technical CS/IT, 12 methodological/practical CS, 18 mathematics and 30 further CS credits excluding the thesis, plus English B2. Algorithms/data structures and a generic discrete-mathematics title do not automatically meet its theoretical-CS definition. [tuberlin2026cs](https://www.tu.berlin/en/studying/study-programs/all-programs-offered/study-course/computer-science-informatik-m-sc) [tuberlin2026csadmission](https://www.tu.berlin/en/eecs/academics-teaching/organizing-your-studies/during-your-studies/msc-computer-science-informatik/msc-cs-in-application-admission)

Nuance: the department separately recognises four logic credits from its specifically named **Discrete Mathematics and Logic** module from winter 2026/27. This is not permission to assign every foreign discrete-mathematics course to theoretical CS; content must be documented. [tuberlin2026theory](https://www.tu.berlin/en/eecs/academics-teaching/organizing-your-studies/during-your-studies/what-is-theoretical-computer-science)

## Assumptions & Uncertainty

- Open for applications is not academically eligible, funded, admitted or enrolled. No offer acceptance, account creation, application submission or scholarship application was performed.
- Annual dates are used as planning rules, labelled as such. Named 2027 announcements are distinguished from inference; programme or portal changes require rechecking before submission.
- The fetched programme/department documents, not university location or an English webpage, establish teaching language. English-only paths and mixed-language degrees are treated differently.
- Course-credit mapping must follow each programme's method. Dresden publishes a conversion rule; that rule must not be silently applied to TU Berlin, Hannover or another university. Credits cannot be double-counted; official learning-outcome evidence can change a provisional title-based allocation.
- Individual certificate-age rules were not verified for every retained programme. A C1 label alone does not prove compliance with an institution's accepted certificates, overall score, component minimums and submission deadline.
- GPA minima are screening thresholds, not admission probabilities. No admission probability is estimated.
- Only the authorised academic/profile summary is incorporated. Original scans, test/student identifiers, addresses, and unrelated applicant documents are excluded.
- Standard tuition-free classifications still involve semester contributions and possible special-case fees. Programme-specific contribution amounts for 2027 were not exhaustively verified.
- A fee waiver is not a living-cost scholarship. Paid programmes remain funding-dependent; a complete secured funding package was not established and scholarship coverage was not exhaustively surveyed.
- HU, Leipzig, Frankfurt HIS, the Dortmund transition, and the UDE medical-informatics lead remain unresolved. These are visible open threads, not confirmed negative findings or verified application opportunities.
