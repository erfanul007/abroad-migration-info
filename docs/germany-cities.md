# German Cities — Job-First Relocation Scoreboard

This is a decision aid for an experienced Bangladeshi software engineer using either an
Opportunity Card or a student-visa route to skilled full-time work in Germany. The app data is the
source of truth; this document mirrors its 21 July 2026 review.

`Conversion & work authorisation` includes registration, the responsible authority, transition
from either current title, and the lawful full-time-work point. A timely application protects
residence under §81(4) AufenthG; full-time work depends on the §81(5a) permission recorded on the
interim certificate.

`City scale & earning power` combines city/metro scale with official BA experienced-software pay
proxies. Job portals and salary analytics corroborate market direction only; portal result counts
are not stored as vacancy totals or sponsorship evidence.

## Berlin-commuter cities

Bernau, Oranienburg and Falkensee are the stronger daily-access options. Brandenburg an der
Havel and Frankfurt (Oder) are longer direct corridors; Cottbus is better treated as a hybrid
commute. Eberswalde is a lower-cost regional-centre option with a longer, less resilient journey.
Always check a dated VBB trip before accepting an office requirement or lease.

## Evidence limits

Official sources do not publish city-level English software-sponsor counts, initial route-change
service levels, newcomer housing availability, or Bangladeshi/Muslim population counts for every
city. Scores remain conservative where that evidence is unavailable. See the research records for
the official expansion audit and the portal/analytics cross-check.
